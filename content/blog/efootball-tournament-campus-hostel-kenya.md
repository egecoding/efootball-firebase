Most campus eFootball tournaments in Kenya run on one shared setup — a TV in the common room, a console that belongs to whoever's block it is, and a rotation of players waiting their turn. It's a completely different problem from a gaming lounge with several consoles running at once: here, everything queues through a single screen, and the class or block WhatsApp group is doing all the coordinating.

## The specific problems a hostel tournament runs into

- **One console, many players.** Someone has to know exactly who plays next, or the queue falls apart the moment two people show up at the same time.
- **Almost everyone is phone-only.** Few students are opening a laptop to check a spreadsheet or a pinned message — whatever tool you use has to work from the phone that's already in their hand.
- **The group turns over every semester.** Nobody wants to teach a new batch of hostel-mates how to use something complicated, and nobody wants to keep maintaining a tool past finals week.

## Setting one up

1. **[Create the tournament](/tournaments/new)** from your phone — pick a format that fits a single-console setup. A straight [knockout keeps things moving fastest](/blog/choosing-the-right-tournament-format) when there's only one screen to share.
2. **Drop the invite link in the block or class group.** Everyone joins by typing their name — [no account needed](/blog/no-account-needed-guest-players), which matters when half the group doesn't want to sign up for one more app just to play a bracket.
3. **The bracket tells everyone who's up next**, so players can check from their own phone instead of hovering around the console asking.
4. **Results get logged with a screenshot**, and the score gets read automatically — useful when a dispute would otherwise mean re-litigating a match in front of half the hostel.

## Why this fits campus better than a general tournament app

A hostel tournament doesn't have a budget, a venue booking, or an admin beyond whoever felt like organizing it that week. It runs entirely from a browser link because that's the only thing guaranteed to work on every player's phone, and it doesn't ask anyone to keep an account past the tournament itself — which matters when the whole group graduates or moves out in a few months anyway.

Whether it's a "FIFA" tournament or an eFootball one on the group chat name, [start yours](/tournaments/new) and send the link to your block before the next free evening.
