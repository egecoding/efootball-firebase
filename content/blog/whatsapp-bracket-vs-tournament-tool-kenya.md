Almost every eFootball tournament in Kenya starts the same way: someone draws a bracket, screenshots it, and posts it in the group. It works for the first round. By round two, it usually doesn't.

Here's where the two approaches actually diverge.

## 1. Keeping track of who plays who

**WhatsApp bracket:** Someone redraws the bracket after every round and reposts the image. If they're offline, busy, or just forget, the next round doesn't happen.

**A real tournament tool:** The bracket updates itself the moment a result comes in. [Nobody has to redraw anything](/blog/how-to-run-a-free-efootball-tournament) — the next round's matchups just appear.

## 2. Settling score disputes

**WhatsApp bracket:** Two players disagree on a scoreline, and it turns into a back-and-forth in the group chat that everyone else has to scroll past.

**A real tournament tool:** Players submit a screenshot of the result screen, and [the score gets read automatically](/blog/ai-screenshot-scanning-and-fair-results) — so the group chat doesn't have to become a courtroom.

## 3. Letting players join

**WhatsApp bracket:** Someone has to manually add every name to the image, which means the organizer becomes a bottleneck the moment more than a few people want in.

**A real tournament tool:** Players [join themselves by tapping a link and typing their name](/blog/no-account-needed-guest-players) — no account, no waiting on an admin to update an image.

## 4. Actually finding the bracket

**WhatsApp bracket:** It's whichever image was posted most recently, buried under however many other messages have come in since.

**A real tournament tool:** One link, always current. Anyone can check the [live standings](/tournaments) at any time without scrolling back through the chat.

## What doesn't change

Both approaches still run entirely through the same WhatsApp group your players already use — [dropping a link in the chat](/blog/efootball-tournaments-kenya-scene) isn't a bigger ask than posting an image was. The difference shows up round two, when one method needs someone to manually keep it updated and the other just doesn't.
